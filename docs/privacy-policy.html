Privacy Policy

Game Deals Watch for Android · Effective 1 November 2026

Game Deals Watch ("the app") helps you track console game prices from the websites xbdeals.net, psdeals.net and ntdeals.net. This policy explains what information the app handles and where it goes. In short: the developer does not collect, receive or sell any of your data. Everything the app stores stays on your device.

1. Information stored on your device

WhatWhyWhere it is kept
Your watchlist (game names, links, prices, target prices)To show your list and send price alertsOnly on your device, in the app's private storage
App settings (refresh time, toggles)To remember your choicesOnly on your device
Website cookies and site data for xbdeals.net, psdeals.net and ntdeals.netTo keep you signed in to those sites inside the appOnly on your device, in Android's WebView storage

The app has no account system of its own and no server. The developer cannot see your watchlist, your settings or your site accounts.

2. Signing in to the deal websites

When you sign in to xbdeals.net, psdeals.net or ntdeals.net, you do so directly on those websites, inside the app's built-in browser. Your email address and password are sent only to the website you are signing in to. The app does not read, store or send your password anywhere else. Those websites handle your account under their own privacy policies.

3. Network connections the app makes

  • The three deal websites, to show their pages, sync your watchlists and wishlists, and check prices. This includes daily background refreshes if you turn them on. These requests carry your site cookies, as any browser would.
  • pgl.yoyo.org, about once a week, to download a public list of advertising servers. It is used for the optional ad-blocking feature. No personal information is sent with this request.

Like any internet request, these connections reveal your device's IP address to the server you contact. All connections use encrypted HTTPS.

4. What the app does not do

  • No analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking SDKs are included.
  • No data is sold, shared with or transferred to the developer or to third parties.
  • The app does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files. Export and import only touch a file you choose.

5. Permissions

  • Internet / network state: to load the deal websites and check prices.
  • Notifications: to alert you about price drops, target prices and daily summaries. You can turn notifications off at any time in Android settings.

6. Your choices and deleting your data

  • Remove games from your watchlist at any time.
  • Settings → Sign out of all sites deletes all website cookies and site data stored by the app.
  • Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes everything the app has stored.
  • Your accounts on the deal websites are managed on those websites. Contact them to change or delete those accounts.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.
